# Role Interview Guide

Participant:
Role:
Workflow area:
Date:

## Opening

- What are you responsible for day to day?
- Which tasks repeat most often?
- Which tasks create the most delay, rework, or stress?

## Recent Work Episode

- Pick one recent task that took too long. What triggered it?
- What happened next?
- What information did you need?
- Where did you look first?
- Which systems, documents, spreadsheets, emails, calls, or people were involved?
- What did you decide, route, approve, create, send, or update?
- Who else touched it?

## Trust and Exceptions

- Which source do you trust most?
- Which source is incomplete, stale, duplicated, or wrong?
- What do you check manually?
- What happens when something is missing or wrong?
- What would a new person get wrong?

## Risk and Value

- What information is sensitive?
- What should require approval?
- What mistake would be expensive, embarrassing, illegal, or damaging?
- If this improved, what would change?
- How would improvement be measured?

## Closing

- What did I not ask that matters?
- Who else should I interview?
- What document/report/example would help explain this?
