# Input Collection Checklist

## Intake

Ask for:

- Business goals.
- Top operational bottlenecks.
- Current systems/tools.
- Current reports/dashboards.
- Painful repeated workflows.
- Sensitive data areas.
- People to interview.
- Definition of success.

## Documents and Examples

Ask for examples, not everything:

- SOPs.
- Current reports.
- Dashboards.
- Spreadsheets.
- Forms.
- Templates.
- Policies.
- Sample tickets/tasks/cases.
- Redacted exports.
- Screenshots or walkthrough videos.

## Systems Inventory

Capture:

- System name.
- Purpose.
- Owner.
- Users.
- Data inside.
- Export/API access.
- Sensitive data.
- Known quality issues.

## Metrics

Ask for:

- Volume.
- Cycle time.
- Response time.
- Error rate.
- Rework.
- Cost.
- Revenue impact.
- Customer impact.
- Risk impact.

## Risk Inputs

Ask:

- What mistake would be expensive?
- What mistake would be embarrassing?
- What mistake would be illegal or compliance-sensitive?
- What should never be automated?
- What requires approval?
