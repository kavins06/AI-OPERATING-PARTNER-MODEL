## Information ecology diagnostic

- Domain: Foundations of information management and organizational intelligence
- Components: People, Process, Culture, Politics, Architecture, Behavior, Technology
- Use cases: Diagnose why information work fails before proposing tools or agents.
- Failure modes: Treating the problem as software-only; ignoring incentives, ownership, or informal workarounds.
- Business application: Map leasing, maintenance, brokerage, diligence, and reporting information flows before automation.

## CDO operating model

- Domain: Data strategy, governance, CDO role, architecture, and Five Vs
- Components: Mandate, Stewardship, Standards, Enablement, Value delivery, Risk management
- Use cases: Create data leadership in organizations that lack mature data functions.
- Failure modes: Unclear decision rights, unfunded governance, and no business-facing value story.
- Business application: Offer fractional data leadership for firms adopting AI agents without an internal CDO.

## Zero Trust reference architecture

- Domain: Privacy, data protection, cybersecurity, zero trust, and regulatory risk
- Components: User, Device, Application/workload, Data, Network/environment, Automation/orchestration, Visibility/analytics
- Use cases: Design access around continuous verification and least privilege.

- Business application: Constrain agents to specific sources, tasks, approval thresholds, and audit trails.
