Capture prompts:
- What does an expert notice that a novice misses?
- What examples show good, bad, and edge-case decisions?
- Which exceptions require escalation?
- Which policies, contracts, laws, or client preferences constrain the work?
- What language should the agent use or avoid?

Outputs:
- Knowledge map.
- Gap analysis.
- Capture backlog.
- Agent-ready SOP and prompt requirements.

Quality checks:
- Tacit judgment is captured with examples, not just labels.
- Documents are linked to the decisions they support.
- Knowledge gaps become implementation tasks before deployment.
