# Principles

The AI operating partner system exists to help enterprises modernize without pretending that automation can safely outrun organizational truth. It turns messy operating reality into structured, validated intelligence before recommending what to build.

## Non-Negotiables

1. The method comes before the app. The webapp should make the method easier to run, review, and govern; it should not quietly replace the method with a shallow intake workflow.
2. Business truth is produced, not merely stored. The system must discover official systems, de facto trusted sources, spreadsheets, macros, manual adjustments, reconciliations, expert judgment, and ownership patterns.
3. Pre-build means pre-build. No agents, MCP servers, connectors, live integrations, production automations, broad data access, or credential requests happen during the engagement.
4. Every recommendation needs traceable evidence. Interviews, redacted artifacts, screenshots, walkthroughs, schemas, source documents, and client confirmations must remain attached to the objects they support.
5. Readiness is behavior-level. A workflow may be ready for read-only summary or draft-and-flag while being unsafe for human-approved action or autonomous action.
6. Hard gates override averages. A high readiness score cannot compensate for unresolved truth production, missing ownership, sensitive-data risk, regulatory exposure, absent controls, or adoption failure.
7. Adoption is part of architecture. Incentives, workflow fit, training, opt-in versus mandate reality, support, feedback, and failure triggers are design requirements, not change-management afterthoughts.
8. Risk controls shape the solution. Privacy, security, output quality, regulated-domain constraints, tool permissions, approval gates, monitoring, incident response, and revocation can force solution redesign.
9. Machine-readable artifacts are the product substrate. The system should produce structured objects that a future app can store, validate, diff, review, export, and hand off.
10. Vertical specificity belongs in extensions. Core method invariants stay stable; domain-specific rules, risks, evidence needs, and lifecycle requirements enter through the vertical-extension contract.
11. Lifecycle is designed before launch. A capability is not build-ready until validation, monitoring, ownership, update, access review, revocation, incident, adoption, expansion, and retirement paths are defined.
12. AI does heavy processing; humans own judgment. AI can interview, extract, synthesize, score, and draft. The operating partner owns scope, prioritization, trust, final recommendation, and client relationship.

## Modernization Thesis

Enterprise modernization fails when it treats AI as a tool-selection problem. The harder problem is making the organization's operating intelligence explicit enough to govern. This system modernizes by creating a reliable bridge from current work reality to future AI-enabled capability design.

The bridge has three parts:

- Discover how work, truth, risk, and adoption actually function.
- Convert that discovery into structured, validated method objects.
- Decide what should be built, remediated, deferred, or avoided.

The future app should make that bridge easier to cross repeatedly.
