# Combined Mastery Capstones

Complete these to prove mastery across both domains.

## Capstone 1: Property Management Maintenance Triage Agent

Deliver:

- Voice-interview synthesis.
- Workflow map from tenant request to resolution.
- Source inventory.
- Data-quality findings.
- Knowledge map of urgency rules, vendor rules, property rules, and escalation criteria.
- Readiness scorecard.
- Warehouse/lakehouse data model sketch.
- Dashboard/KPI plan.
- Privacy/security risk register.
- Pilot design and validation plan.

Acceptance:

- Emergency escalation is explicit.
- Sensitive tenant data is controlled.
- Vendor dispatch is approval-gated unless client policy allows otherwise.
- Success metric includes triage speed, SLA compliance, and misroute rate.

## Capstone 2: Residential Brokerage Lead Routing and Follow-Up Agent

Deliver:

- Business outcome and workflow owner.
- CRM/listing/source map.
- Data governance model.
- Approved outreach language.
- Fair Housing-sensitive guardrails.
- Lead scoring or prioritization logic with uncertainty.
- Human approval path.
- Dashboard for speed-to-lead, conversion, appointment rate, and agent acceptance.

Acceptance:

- No protected-class inference or targeting.
- Opt-out and consent handling are named.
- Routing rules are explainable and auditable.
- Current legal/compliance claims are verified before external use.

## Capstone 3: CRE Acquisition Screening and Diligence Assistant

Deliver:

- Acquisition-screening workflow map.
- Offering memo and diligence-room document inventory.
- Data extraction and citation strategy.
- Comp and market-data validation plan.
- Assumption log.
- Investment committee review model.
- KPI and dashboard standard.
- Risk register.

Acceptance:

- No investment conclusion is autonomous.
- Every deal fact can be traced to a source.
- Assumptions are separated from facts.
- Current market and legal claims are verified before client-facing delivery.

## Capstone 4: Investor Reporting Agent

Deliver:

- Source-of-record map for GL, rent roll, occupancy, maintenance, leasing, and capex.
- KPI dictionary.
- Semantic layer sketch.
- Reporting workflow map.
- Narrative drafting rules.
- Finance approval workflow.
- Audit log requirements.
- Dashboard and exception-review plan.

Acceptance:

- Finance-approved definitions control all metrics.
- Agent drafts are source-linked and reviewed.
- Sensitive investor data has access boundaries.
- Success metric includes reporting cycle time, correction rate, and stakeholder satisfaction.

## Capstone 5: Segment-Agnostic Managed-Agent Operating Model

Deliver:

- Intake form.
- Discovery interview process.
- Readiness scorecard.
- Data/knowledge/governance/risk artifact templates.
- Build, validate, deploy, operate, improve SOP.
- Monitoring dashboard design.
- Incident and escalation path.
- Quarterly improvement cadence.

Acceptance:

- Works across property management, brokerage, CRE, development, lending, and investor reporting.
- Treats agents as governed information systems.
- Includes ownership, controls, measurement, and improvement loops.
- Names stop conditions for unsafe automation.
