# Cases and Operating Lessons

Purpose: Extract transferable lessons from business, government, intelligence, health care, cyber, and business cases.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain and apply case studies and operating lessons.
- Convert source concepts into business AI-agent decisions, controls, and templates.
- Distinguish technology problems from data, information, knowledge, governance, privacy, and adoption problems.

## Practice Drills

- Teach the module to a skeptical business operator in 7 minutes using only source-grounded claims.
- Pick one property-management, one brokerage, and one CRE workflow; identify the data, information, knowledge, privacy, and governance work required before automation.
- Write a one-page client memo that separates facts from assumptions and names the source evidence.

## Quiz

- Q: What decisions or controls does case studies and operating lessons require before deploying an AI agent?
  A: Define the business outcome, source systems, ownership, quality expectations, access rules, risk controls, human oversight, and success metrics before automation.
- Q: How would you turn this module into an operator-grade client deliverable?
  A: Convert concepts into a discovery checklist, risk register, data/source inventory, governance decisions, implementation SOP, and measurement plan with source-backed rationale.
