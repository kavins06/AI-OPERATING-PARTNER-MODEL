# Managed AI-Agent Lifecycle SOP

Purpose: manage AI agents as living information systems, not one-time automations.

Lifecycle:
1. Intake: business outcome, owner, workflow, sources, risk class.
2. Readiness: score value, workflow clarity, data, knowledge, privacy/security, integration, oversight, measurement.
3. Design: permissions, prompts, retrieval sources, tools, escalation, logs, tests.
4. Build: sandbox first, synthetic and historical examples, human review.
5. Validate: source-grounding, edge cases, privacy/security controls, business metrics.
6. Deploy: staged rollout, owner training, incident path.
7. Operate: monitor errors, drift, user feedback, data quality, and risk events.
8. Improve: refresh sources, update SOPs, retire unused flows, expand only after evidence.

Quality checks:
- The agent has a named business owner and technical owner.
- Source systems and permissions are documented.
- Human-in-the-loop rules are explicit.
- Logs support audit and improvement without exposing unnecessary sensitive data.
