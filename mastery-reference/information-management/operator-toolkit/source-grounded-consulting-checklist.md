# Source-Grounded Consulting Checklist

Purpose: keep client advice traceable to the corpus and current official sources where needed.

Before giving advice:
- Name the client workflow and decision.
- Identify the source concept or framework being applied.
- Link the claim to source notes, page/slide/block references, or current official guidance.
- Separate facts, assumptions, and recommendations.
- Check whether the claim depends on current law, regulation, security standards, vendor policy, or market facts.
- If current verification is needed, verify before making client-facing claims.

Deliverable standard:
- Every recommendation has a business reason.
- Every automation has a data/knowledge prerequisite.
- Every data access has a privacy/security control.
- Every pilot has a measure of success.
