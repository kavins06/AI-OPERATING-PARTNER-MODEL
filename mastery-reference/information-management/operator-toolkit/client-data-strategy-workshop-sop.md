# Client Data Strategy Workshop SOP

Purpose: translate business goals into data and AI-agent priorities.

When to use: before roadmap creation, pilot selection, or managed-agent onboarding.

Required inputs:
- Business goals and pain points.
- System inventory.
- Data/source inventory.
- Current reports, dashboards, SOPs, and exception examples.
- Compliance and client-service constraints.

Process:
1. Establish business outcomes: revenue, retention, vacancy, response time, diligence speed, reporting quality, risk reduction.
2. Identify decisions and workflows that depend on information.
3. Map sources of record, shadow spreadsheets, documents, email, and tribal knowledge.
4. Score data using the Five Vs: volume, velocity, variety, veracity, value.
5. Define governance: owners, definitions, quality rules, access, retention, escalation.
6. Rank AI-agent use cases by value, readiness, risk, and implementation effort.
7. Choose one pilot and one enabling data-management fix.

Outputs:
- Data strategy one-pager.
- Prioritized AI-agent roadmap.
- Source inventory and governance decisions.
- Pilot success metrics.

Quality checks:
- Every AI idea links to a business outcome.
- Every business outcome links to required data and knowledge.
- Risks are handled before implementation scope is finalized.
