# Combined Mastery Curriculum

Goal: operator-level mastery across data analytics and information management for business AI-agent delivery.

Mastery rule: do not move to client build work until you can produce source-grounded answers, workflow maps, readiness scores, risk controls, and measurement plans without tool-first thinking.

## Module 1: Information as an Operating System

Master:

- Information management.
- Information ecology.
- Sensemaking.
- Organizational decision making.
- Environmental scanning.
- Data, information, knowledge, and action.

Proof:

- Explain why an AI-agent project is not just a software project.
- Map a business workflow as an information ecology.
