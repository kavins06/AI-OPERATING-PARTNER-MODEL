# Section 1: Discovery / Pre-Build

This section is the AI Operating Partner Engagement: the Step 0-18 process that discovers reality, validates the workflow/data/truth layer, designs the future AI capability, scores readiness, produces the implementation decision packet, and ends with the Step 18 lifecycle and method-to-build handoff.

This section does not build agents, tools, connectors, live integrations, MCP servers, normalization pipelines, or production automations.

## Primary Skill

- `../skills/ai-operating-partner-engagement`

## What It Contains

- Step 0: engagement tiering and vertical extension.
- Steps 1-2: executive terrain and candidate use-case shortlist.
- Steps 3-5: interviews, variation mapping, and planning-evidence request.
- Steps 6-8: workflow intelligence, diagnostic, governance validation, and baseline release.
- Steps 9-10: knowledge requirements and AI guidance/eval pack.
- Steps 11-12: measurement intelligence and value case.
- Steps 13-15: solution shape, adoption design, technical blueprint, and risk/control model.
- Steps 16-18: readiness score, implementation decision packet, managed lifecycle, and handoff.
